## Art Direction
Desired:
- Sophisticated, human-designed, editorial, minimal, confident
- Strong typography and intentional whitespace
- Technical without cliché
- Restrained yellow accent

Avoid:
- Generic AI portfolio layouts
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Neon gradients
- Floating AI dashboards/cards
- Fake AI popups, assistant widgets or status badges
- Fake skill percentages
- Giant gradient blobs
- Excessive rounded cards
- Over-animation
- Decorative elements without purpose

## Hero
The hero is the visual priority.

**Left:** small professional eyebrow, large “Asad Chaudhary”, strong developer/value statement, concise paragraph, Hire Me and View Work.

**Right:** owner-provided profile photograph in a clean editorial frame.

Allowed near photo: thin line, subtle yellow accent, simple geometric frame or controlled crop.

Forbidden near/on photo:
- AI popup
- Chat bubble
- Fake browser window
- Analytics panel
- Skill bubble
- Notification
- “AI” or “Available” badge
- Floating UI cards
- Anything covering the face/photo

The photograph must remain clean and prominent.
